Five narrow agents beat one large prompt: each can fail, retry, and be tiered independently.

Benchmark:97% pipeline completion across 100 test sources; 20/20 reviewed citations grounded correctly, zero fabricated.
Model Tiering & Cost Modeling

Cost per unit is a product metric. If you can't state it, you can't price it.

Evaluation Harnesses

An accuracy claim without a labeled test set is an opinion.

Proof:Built a harness scoring extraction against hand-labeled ground truth: 100% recall/precision on 55 line items, 2 real quotes.
